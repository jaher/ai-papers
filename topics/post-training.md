# Post-Training

Everything after pre-training: instruction tuning, RLHF/RLAIF, preference optimization, and RL training methods that turn base models into assistants and reasoners.

| Paper | arXiv | Authors | Added | Summary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback | [2203.02155](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155) | Long Ouyang et al. | 2026-09-30 | Making language models bigger does not inherently make them better at following a user's intent |
| Scaling Instruction-Finetuned Language Models | [2210.11416](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.11416) | Hyung Won Chung et al. | 2026-09-30 | Finetuning language models on a collection of datasets phrased as instructions has been shown to improve model performance and generalization to unseen tasks |
| Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback | [2212.08073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073) | Yuntao Bai et al. | 2026-10-04 | We experiment with methods for training a harmless AI assistant through self-improvement, without any human labels identifying harmful outputs |
| Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model | [2305.18290](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290) | Rafael Rafailov et al. | 2026-10-04 | While large-scale unsupervised language models learn broad world knowledge and reasoning skills during pretraining, achieving precise control over their behavior is difficult due to the unsupervised nature of this pretraining |
| Learning to Solve Hard Problems in RL for LLMs by Never Giving Up | [2609.13443](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13443) | Michael Noukhovitch, Hamish Ivison, Nathan Lambert, Aaron Courville | 2026-10-04 | RL improves LLMs mostly on easy problems (a Matthew effect); NGU keeps sampling until a correct solution appears, shifting compute to hard problems |
