# KV-Cache Eviction & Management

Papers on KV-cache eviction scoring, adaptive budgets, and management policies — which entries to keep and how much memory to give each request.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| DeferKV: Rethinking Eviction Timing for One-Shot KV Cache Compression | [2610.06286](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06286) | Zhe Wang, Jiakai Li, Yujia Sun, Rongzheng Wang, Shuang Liang | 2026-10-06 | Moves the one-shot eviction decision from end-of-prefill to the first real decoding step, combining prompt-side and decode-side attention observations — early decode queries match later decode attention better than prefill-only signals. Training-free, no draft model or future-query predictor; improves compressed-KV quality on LongBench, RULER, and Needle-in-a-Haystack at low added latency. |
| ValueDiff: Value-Geometric KV Cache Eviction for Sink-Suppressed LLMs | [2609.23314](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23314) | Junyoung Park, Jungwook Choi, Mingu Lee | 2026-10-01 | Ranks eviction candidates by L2 deviation of value vectors from the cache mean — a value-geometry signal (minimal-disturbance under max-entropy future attention) for sink-suppressed models (QK-norm, gated attention, logit softcapping). RULER 2k budget: 88–99% of dense across 7 models (best on 6/7); LongBench 4k: 92% vs 83% prior best; MATH-500 strongest non-dense at 25% cache on gated-attention models. |
| Risk-Controlled KV-Cache Eviction: From Memory Budgets to Risk Targets | [2609.27981](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27981) | Beomgu Kang, SoJin Yun, Hojoon Kim, Hyunseok Seo | 2026-10-01 | Reformulates eviction as a deployment risk-control problem: material degradation = utility drop beyond a tolerance vs full-KV; a compressor-agnostic post-hoc certification picks a retention policy from calibration data with a finite-sample guarantee, falling back to full KV when nothing certifies. On Llama it certifies SnapKV at 75% retention on LongBench but no compressed policy on RULER-32K (fallback). Turns a reliability contract into a KV-memory operating point. |
| ActKV: Efficient LLM Agents through Action-Guided KV Cache Management | [2609.31395](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31395) | Zihan Wang et al. | 2026-09-29 | First KV-cache compression framework tailored for agentic LLM inference: action-oriented eviction retains entries critical to future *action* generation; confidence-driven adaptive budget allocation grows/shrinks per-request KV budget with model confidence; page-aware compression with customized kernels plugs into paged-memory serving. 98.53% of FullKV accuracy with 25.98% of its peak KV memory; 3.97x token / 3.58x task throughput vs FullKV. |
| Beyond Mean Attention: Diversity-Aware, Layer-Wise Scoring for KV Cache Eviction | [2609.30738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30738) | Tianfang Xie, Wei Zhu | 2026-09-30 | Eviction scoring beyond mean attention: score = μ_i + λ1σ_i + λ2·corr(i,S) — attention dispersion plus redundancy vs already-selected tokens (MMR-style, no extra forward passes). On 16 LongBench datasets at 64 entries/layer, a single global diversification constant improves 13/16; per-dataset search finds a mid-layer sign flip on passage retrieval worth +9.6. A cheap drop-in for SnapKV/PyramidKV-style eviction stacks. |
